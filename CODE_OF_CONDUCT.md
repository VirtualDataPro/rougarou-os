# Community conduct

Rougarou OS welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of Linux
experience. This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests
and reviews.

Be respectful, explain technical disagreements and criticize the proposed change.
Help newcomers reproduce problems and keep discussions relevant to Rougarou.
Respect other people's privacy and ask before sharing their personal information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, deliberate
personal attacks, spam and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner, `VirtualDataPro`, through a
private contact method listed on their GitHub profile if available. Do not publish
sensitive details in an issue or discussion. If no private contact is available,
use GitHub's reporting tools to report abusive content.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock conversations and
restrict participation. They will consider severity, context and repeated behavior.
